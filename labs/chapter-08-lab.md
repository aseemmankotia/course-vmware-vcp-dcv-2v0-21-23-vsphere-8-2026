# Chapter 8 Lab: Snapshots, Clones, and vMotion/Storage vMotion

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Take a snapshot of a running VM, generate disk changes, then consolidate the snapshot; separately perform a Storage vMotion of a powered-on VM between two datastores and observe the migration wizard's compatibility checks

## Key takeaway

Snapshots, clones, and vMotion each solve different operational problems, and the exam expects precise knowledge of prerequisites like EVC and shared storage for live migration scenarios.
