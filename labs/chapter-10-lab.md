# Chapter 10 Lab: vSphere HA, Fault Tolerance, and Admission Control

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Configure a vSphere HA cluster with a percentage-based admission control policy, set the host isolation response to 'Power off and restart VMs', then enable Fault Tolerance on a single-vCPU test VM and observe the secondary VM creation

## Key takeaway

Admission control policy choice determines cluster capacity headroom, and the exam distinguishes HA's restart-based recovery from FT's true zero-downtime protection based on the stated availability requirement.
