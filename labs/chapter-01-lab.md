# Chapter 1 Lab: vSphere 8 Architecture and ESXi Installation

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Install ESXi 8 in a nested lab VM using the interactive installer, then configure management networking and NTP through the DCUI and Host Client

## Key takeaway

ESXi is the hypervisor foundation of every vSphere deployment, and the exam expects you to match installation and management methods to specific scale and connectivity scenarios.
