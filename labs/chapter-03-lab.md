# Chapter 3 Lab: Standard and Distributed Virtual Switches

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Create a vSphere Distributed Switch spanning two hosts, configure a distributed port group with VLAN tagging, then migrate a running VM's network adapter from a standard switch to the vDS

## Key takeaway

Choose vDS over vSS whenever centralized management, NIOC, or private VLANs are required, and match VLAN tagging mode precisely to the described traffic-handling scenario.
