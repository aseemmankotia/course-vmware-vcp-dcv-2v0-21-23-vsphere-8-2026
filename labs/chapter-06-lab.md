# Chapter 6 Lab: vSAN and Storage Policy-Based Management (SPBM)

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Create a storage policy defining FTT=1 with RAID-1 mirroring, apply it to a test VM on a vSAN datastore, then compare capacity consumption against a RAID-5 erasure coding policy

## Key takeaway

vSAN and SPBM shift storage decisions from LUN provisioning to policy assignment, and the exam tests whether you can match FTT/RAID choices and host counts to availability and capacity requirements.
